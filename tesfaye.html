My Hobbies & Activities

One of the most important things I do outside of soccer is spending time with friends. It helps me relax, recharge, and remember that life isn't all about performance or pressure. God created us for community, and being around people who uplift me keeps me grounded in my faith.

Playing soccer has always been more than just a sport for me—it's a way I connect with God. On the field, I feel free. It's where I can push myself, use the gifts God gave me, and honor Him through discipline and teamwork.

Another big part of my life is volunteering in my church community. Whether I'm helping with youth events, setting up for services, or mentoring younger kids, it brings me joy to serve.
