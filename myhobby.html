Soccer - More Than Just a Game

Playing soccer has always been more than just a sport for me—it's a way I connect with God. On the field, I feel free. It's where I can push myself, use the gifts God gave me, and honor Him through discipline and teamwork. Soccer teaches humility, perseverance, and trust—qualities that also help me grow spiritually.

Every game is a reminder that I play for an audience of One. Whether we're winning or losing, it's about giving my best and using the talents I've been blessed with. The community and friendships I've built through soccer have been invaluable, showing me how sports can bring people together and create opportunities to share faith through actions.
